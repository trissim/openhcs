\section{Artifact Index and Supplementary Tables}\label{app:lean}

This paper uses the same theorem-local proof-handle convention as the rest of the series: inline markers of the form \verb|\LH{...}| and \verb|\LHrng{...}| attach formal support directly to the corresponding paper claims.

The cited-handle closure comprises \LeanReleaseLines{} lines and \LeanReleaseTheorems{} theorem-level declarations across \LeanReleaseFiles{} files; this is the code needed for the handles cited in the paper. The local development contains \LeanLocalLines{} lines and \LeanLocalTheorems{} theorem-level declarations across \LeanLocalFiles{} files, while the cumulative transitive dependency closure contains \LeanTotalLines{} lines and \LeanTotalTheorems{} theorem-level declarations across \LeanTotalFiles{} files. Thus the local development contributes \number\numexpr 100*\LeanLocalLines/\LeanTotalLines\relax\% of cumulative lines and \number\numexpr 100*\LeanLocalTheorems/\LeanTotalTheorems\relax\% of cumulative theorem count. The supplementary handle ledger records source-file provenance for every cited handle: entries under \texttt{Frontier/*} are newly introduced here, whereas entries under \texttt{DecisionQuotient/*} are inherited from an earlier decision-quotient development.

The full handle ledger and claim-to-handle mapping are provided in the supplementary PDF and archived artifact. We omit those longer tables from the manuscript body.
