\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
%==============================================================================

Exact consistency in modifiable multi-location encodings yields a deterministic finite converse family. Once the observation transcript leaves a nontrivial ambiguity class, the same finite obstruction can be stated as a confusability-clique bound, a counting converse, a conditional-entropy lower bound, and decoder-output or finite-gap constraints. The same model also supports deterministic data-processing and a budgeted finite-error extension.

The multi-fact partial-observation extension shows that the theory is not confined to clique-shaped ambiguity classes. In that richer regime, exact recovery becomes a colorability problem, exactness on a success set becomes colorability of an induced subgraph, the exact finite weighted-success value is determined by the largest colorable induced subgraph, the full $n$-block confusability graph is identified with the $n$-fold strong power of the one-shot confusability graph, and the resulting block-rate sequence carries a supermultiplicative lower-capacity envelope equal to the corresponding graph-side strong-power expression, together with an $n$-block exponential lower bound for one-shot zero-error code size. The same strong-power sequence is now also shown to converge to a real asymptotic capacity value via Fekete's lemma, with every finite block rate bounded above by that limit, the limit itself identified with the supremum of the finite block-rate sequence, and the entire asymptotic capacity bounded above by the logarithm of the complement chromatic number.\leanmeta{\LH{MFT25}}\leanmeta{\LH{MFT30}}\leanmeta{\LH{MFT35}}\leanmeta{\LH{MFT36}}\leanmeta{\LH{MFT44}}\leanmeta{\LH{MFT45}}\leanmeta{\LH{MFT48}}\leanmeta{\LH{MFT49}}\leanmeta{\LH{MFT55}}

The threshold $C_0=1$ is the structural corollary of that converse family: one authoritative location with deterministic views is the unique zero-incoherence regime, and any higher independent rate makes ambiguity reachable. The same obstruction then reappears operationally as manual update cost, yielding $O(1)$ cost at rate $1$ and $\Omega(n)$ cost above it, with an unbounded asymptotic gap. The realizability theorem identifies causal propagation and provenance observability as the host-level conditions under which the rate-$1$ regime is actually attained.

\textbf{Limitations and future work.} The present model is still finite and deterministic. The new graph-theoretic extension reaches non-clique confusability, success-set colorability, and an explicit supermultiplicative block-growth scaffold, but it does not yet supply a full Shannon-capacity or Lov\'asz-theta theory, nor a complete exact rate-distortion characterization for the extended model. Those are the next natural targets.
