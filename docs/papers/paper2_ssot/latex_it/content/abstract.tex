\begin{abstract}
We study exact consistency in finite multi-location encodings, where one latent value is represented at several writable or derived locations and an observer sees a deterministic transcript of the current state. The core result is a deterministic finite converse family: exact zero-error recovery from observation and auxiliary tags is equivalent to injectivity of the induced observation-tag map, and the same obstruction yields confusability, counting, conditional-entropy, decoder-output, and finite-gap formulations. In particular, a surviving $k$-way ambiguity class forces at least $\log_2 k$ bits of side information. The same model also supports deterministic data processing and a budgeted finite-error extension.

We then extend the model to multi-fact latent tuples with partial observations, where the confusability graph becomes the structural object controlling exact consistency under partial views and need not be clique-shaped. In that regime, exact recovery is equivalent to graph colorability, exact recovery on a success set is equivalent to colorability of the induced subgraph, and the exact finite weighted-success value is determined by the maximum $T$-colorable induced subgraph. Block-composed view families induce the strong powers of the one-shot confusability graph, yielding a real asymptotic Shannon-capacity theorem on the lower side together with upper bounds by the complement chromatic number and by a fixed textbook Lov\'asz-$\vartheta$ convention. The same one-shot upper invariant is identified with standard orthonormal-representation, primal-PSD, and dual-theta forms, and the corresponding asymptotic primal and dual theta upper sequences converge to the same upper value.

The same mechanized theory proves exact equality on the cluster-collapse subclass: asymptotic Shannon capacity and the fixed Lov\'asz-$\vartheta$ upper both collapse to the logarithm of the number of confusability components. For the original view-family model, that collapse occurs exactly when base confusability is transitive; a checkable meet-witness condition is sufficient; and fiber coherence is a stronger sufficient condition under which the common value reduces to the logarithm of realized transcript fibers. Under a separate affine restriction on the realized state family, the fact side carries its own representable matroid: semantic determination of one fact by others is equivalent to span membership of the corresponding coordinate functional, and minimal determining fact sets are exactly the matroid bases. As a threshold corollary, the largest independent rate compatible with zero incoherence is exactly $1$: one authoritative source with deterministic views achieves exact consistency, whereas more than one independently writable source makes ambiguity reachable. A realizability theorem identifies causal propagation and provenance observability as the host-level conditions for attaining that regime, and the threshold yields $O(1)$ manual update cost at rate $1$ and $\Omega(n)$ cost above it. A supplementary Lean 4 artifact machine-checks the converse family, graph extension, threshold chain, realizability theorem, asymptotic upper theory, and affine fact-matroid theorem. \textbf{Index Terms:} zero-error information theory, side information, confusability, exact consistency, deterministic converse
\end{abstract}
