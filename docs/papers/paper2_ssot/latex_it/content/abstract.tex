\begin{abstract}
We study exact recovery in finite multi-location encodings through a deterministic zero-error model with latent states, observation transcripts, and auxiliary tags. A multi-fact partial-view extension induces confusability graphs on latent tuples that need not be cliques. In this model, exact recovery with a $T$-ary tag is equivalent to $T$-colorability of the induced graph, exact recovery on a designated success set is equivalent to colorability of the induced subgraph, and the optimal finite weighted success value is the maximum source mass of a $T$-colorable induced subgraph.

Repeated composition lifts this graph model to strong graph powers: the full $n$-block confusability graph is the $n$-fold strong power of the one-shot graph, the normalized block-rate sequence converges to a real asymptotic Shannon-capacity value equal to the supremum of the finite block rates, and that capacity is bounded above both by the complement chromatic number and by a fixed Lov\'asz-$\vartheta$ upper bound. The same upper invariant admits standard orthonormal-representation, primal-PSD, and dual-theta forms.

The paper also proves an equality characterization. On the cluster-collapse subclass, asymptotic Shannon capacity and the fixed Lov\'asz-$\vartheta$ upper both equal the logarithm of the number of confusability components. For the original view-family model, transitivity of base confusability is the exact sharpness criterion; a meet-witness condition is sufficient; and fiber coherence is a stronger sufficient condition that sharpens the value to the logarithm of realized transcript fibers. Under an affine restriction on the realized state family, the same framework also yields a fact-side representable matroid: semantic determination is span membership of coordinate functionals, and the minimal determining fact sets are exactly the bases.

The deterministic foundation is a finite converse: exact zero-error recovery is equivalent to injectivity of the observation-tag map, and the same obstruction appears in counting and entropy forms together with deterministic data processing and a budgeted finite-error extension. As downstream consequences, the framework also yields a realizability criterion for concrete hosts based on causal propagation and provenance observability. A supplementary Lean 4 artifact machine-checks the converse, graph, equality, affine, and realizability chains. \textbf{Index Terms:} zero-error information theory, side information, confusability, exact consistency, deterministic converse
\end{abstract}
