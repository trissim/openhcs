\begin{abstract}
We study exact consistency in finite multi-location encodings, where one latent value is represented at several writable or derived locations and an observer sees a deterministic transcript of the current state. Our main contribution is a deterministic finite converse family: exact zero-error recovery from observation and auxiliary tags is equivalent to injectivity of the induced observation-tag map, and this same obstruction yields confusability, counting, conditional-entropy, decoder-output, and finite-gap formulations. In particular, if the observation transcript leaves a $k$-way ambiguity class, any zero-error resolver requires at least $\log_2 k$ bits of side information. The same model also supports deterministic data processing and a budgeted finite-error extension. A second mechanized layer extends the model to multi-fact latent tuples with partial observations, where confusability need not be clique-shaped: exact recovery becomes equivalent to graph colorability, exact recovery on a success set is equivalent to colorability of the induced subgraph, the exact finite weighted-success value is determined by the maximum $T$-colorable induced subgraph, and block-composed view families satisfy matching multiplicative coloring and clique-budget laws. In that extension, the full $n$-block confusability graph is identified with the $n$-fold strong power of the one-shot confusability graph, the normalized block-rate sequence admits a genuine asymptotic limit obtained from Fekete's lemma, and the same asymptotic capacity is bounded above both by the logarithm of the complement chromatic number and by a fixed textbook Lov\'asz-$\vartheta$ upper convention. The one-shot upper invariant is identified with standard orthonormal-representation, primal-PSD, and dual-theta forms; the primal and dual standard theta upper objects are proved equal in that convention; and the corresponding strong-power sequences converge to the same asymptotic upper value. Thus the extended model now has an exact one-shot weighted characterization, a real asymptotic capacity theorem, and a mechanized primal-dual Lov\'asz-$\vartheta$ upper theory. As a threshold corollary, the largest independent rate compatible with zero incoherence is exactly $1$: one authoritative source with deterministic views achieves exact consistency, whereas more than one independently writable source makes ambiguity reachable. A realizability theorem identifies causal propagation and provenance observability as the host-level conditions for attaining that regime, and the threshold has an operational consequence for updates, giving $O(1)$ manual modification cost at rate $1$ and $\Omega(n)$ cost above it. A supplementary Lean 4 artifact machine-checks the converse family, the multi-fact graph extension, the threshold chain, the realizability theorem, and the rate-complexity arguments. \textbf{Index Terms:} zero-error information theory, side information, confusability, exact consistency, deterministic converse
\end{abstract}
