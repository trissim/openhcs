\section{System Classification}\label{sec:evaluation}

Corollary~\ref{ssot-iff} states that verifiable structural integrity holds iff both causal propagation and provenance observability hold. We apply this criterion uniformly to representative systems. Complete classification with full justifications appears in Appendix~\ref{sec:appendix-classification}.

The key finding is that the majority of common infrastructure falls into patterns that cannot verify integrity. This is not merely a maintenance inconvenience but an architectural exposure: the host cannot distinguish valid derived states from undetected stale states through its own structural interface.
