\subsection{Limitations}

\textbf{1. Independence Assumption:} The probability model treats DOF-level errors as independent under the orthogonality assumptions from \nolinkurl{AbstractClassSystem}~\cite{paper1_typing_discipline}. Real systems may have correlated failure modes not captured by the isomorphism.

\textbf{2. Constant Error Rate:} Assumes $p$ is uniform across components. Some components are more error-prone than others; a weighted version is future work.

\textbf{3. P~$\neq$~coNP Hypothesis (resolved):} The thermodynamic energy bound in Theorem~5.7, point~3 is now proved unconditionally (\LH{L55}). Point~2 (complexity hardness) holds under P~$\neq$~coNP, which follows from the physical axioms of \cite{paper4_decision_quotient} (\LH{LP38}).

\textbf{4. Capability Quantification:} The leverage side still uses qualitative capability counting. That is adequate for the equivalence theorem but not yet the final word for weighted physical or engineering valuations.

\subsection{Impact}

\textbf{For mathematical physics:} the England Replication Inequality (Theorem~\ref{thm:england}) is reduced to finite counting plus Landauer calibration. The result gives a mechanized, non-asymptotic entropy gap for replication without appealing to an informal thermodynamic analogy.

\textbf{For information theory and complexity:} the paper identifies structural rank as the information coordinate of DOF and shows that the minimum-cost thermodynamic regime coincides with the tractable sufficiency regime.

\textbf{For formalized theory building:} the argument is an example of cross-paper theorem transport in Lean: a structural parameter introduced in one theorem family is lifted into complexity and physics in another, and the full equivalence remains auditable at the proof-artifact level.

\subsection{Final Remarks}

This paper proves finite thermodynamic and structural consequences of the single-source condition from first principles. The regime DOF~$=1$ is not merely a software heuristic; it is the unique point at which leverage, coherence, structural rank, tractable sufficiency, and minimum calibrated decision cost coincide.

All theorems are machine-checked. The thermodynamic lower bound is unconditional, and the replication inequality gives the entropy premium for multiplicity as $k_B \ln k$ per cycle. The companion software-engineering split develops the architectural and case-study implications separately; the present manuscript isolates the mathematical-physics core.
