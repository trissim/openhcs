\section{Introduction}\label{introduction}

Molecular docking lacks a rigorous theory of the exact object its methods target. Exact configuration resolution under constraints supplies that object. The structural statements are finite counting, coordinate sufficiency, structural rank, decision-quotient entropy, certification hardness, and thermodynamic lower bounds. The abstract layer is a bounded decision system, represented in Lean by \texttt{Architecture} together with the canonical binary encoding \texttt{canonicalDP}. The molecular layer instantiates the same framework through holonomic constraint topology, cutoff-local interaction structure, sampled docking families, and concrete scorer families. The quotient object is closer to zero-error and confusability-based information than to average-case coding \cite{shannon1956zero,korner1973graphs,lovasz1979shannon}.

One exact-resolution spine controls the theorem package. The decision quotient fixes the coarsest exact abstraction. Structural rank fixes the irreducible dimension of that quotient. The same rank controls quotient entropy, Fisher-information dimension, certification burden, and Landauer cost. For constrained molecular systems with $N$ atoms and $k$ independent holonomic constraints, the transported degree-of-freedom count is $3N-k$, and the exact-resolution floor scales with that remaining unconstrained dimension. Every docking pipeline therefore either solves this object, approximates it, or replaces it with a surrogate objective.

The theorem package extends the rank-entropy-cost chain. Surjective abstractions either factor through the decision quotient or erase a decision-relevant distinction, and any physically feasible surjective collapse must therefore factor through the quotient. Any sound checker for the empty-set hardness core requires witness budget at least $2^{n-1}$. The same structural rank is also the exact Fisher-information dimension of the decision problem. Continuous-to-grid resolution control yields uniform exact/coarse approximation and strict-gap winner preservation. Lattice-tail bounds discharge the docking cutoff hypothesis and compose directly into structural-rank bounds for exact Lennard-Jones, Coulomb, and real-space Ewald docking families. Finite sampled domains admit canonical discrepancy radii, bounded action families admit polynomial-time exact checking, action-side symmetry breaking yields a canonical strict sampled winner with a uniform positive strict gap and no structural-rank increase whenever at least two sampled actions remain, cutoff locality, sampled exact/coarse invariance, top-$k$ survivor control, ambiguity-band containment, strict-dominance and multiplicative-separability collapse regimes, and concrete Lennard-Jones, Coulomb, and Ewald theorems transport the abstract object into a molecular docking theory with explicit scorer families, approximation regimes, certificates, and geometric execution structure.

\subsection{Theorem Package}

The endpoint of the theory is the convergence theorem in Section~\ref{five-way-equivalence}:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\underbrace{\mathrm{DOF}(A) = 1}_{\text{one-coordinate regime}}
\;\iff\;
\underbrace{\mathrm{srank}(\mathrm{canonicalDP}(A)) = 1}_{\text{structural rank}} \\
&\iff\;
\underbrace{\text{tractable sufficiency for } \mathrm{canonicalDP}(A)}_{\text{complexity}}
\;\iff\;
\underbrace{\text{minimum per-cycle thermodynamic cost}}_{\text{physics}}
\end{aligned}
\]

An imported coherence theorem gives a separate single-source reading of the same rank-$1$ point:
\[
\mathrm{SSOT}(A) \iff \mathrm{DOF}(A)=1,
\]
where $\mathrm{SSOT}(A)$ denotes the coherent single-source condition that one locus is authoritative, every remaining encoding is a derived view, and all reachable states remain coherent.

The convergence theorem is the endpoint of the theorem chain. Earlier sections establish four ingredients that make the equivalence nontrivial: the quotient boundary for exact abstractions, the identification of structural rank with both irreducible coordinate count and Fisher-information dimension, the qualitative and quantitative certification lower bounds, and the molecular locality and stability theorems that make the docking specialization concrete.

Exact molecular resolution requires a sufficient coordinate set. Cutoff locality bounds the number of decision-relevant protein coordinates by the active site together with the ligand coordinates. Sampled docking preserves exact winners under explicit half-gap control, and an action-side symmetry-broken flat refinement canonically chooses a strict sampled winner with a uniform positive strict gap over the finite grid whenever the sampled action family has size at least two. The same sampled layer retains exact top-$k$ survivors under a certified boundary gap and admits a near-tie ambiguity band when strict separation fails. The resulting structural-rank bounds identify low-rank exact docking regimes. Finite flat grid encodings also carry a direct structural-rank entropy bound, and Section~\ref{main-theorems} now applies the thermodynamic floor directly to the exact docking decision problem, to an explicit fixed-length binary encoding of the finite sampled grid problem, and to the bounded-region sampled inside-cutoff process witness under explicit sufficient-set and budget hypotheses. Together these give the concrete molecular instantiation of the abstract theory.

\subsection{Contributions}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Reference Semantics for Molecular Docking:} exact molecular docking is exact configuration resolution under constraints through sufficient coordinate sets, the decision quotient, and the canonical exact-resolution encoding. Surjective exact summaries factor through the quotient, while any extra collapse erases a decision-relevant distinction. Approximate and heuristic docking methods are thereby located relative to a single exact target object.

\item \textbf{Complexity Boundary:} general exact sufficiency certification contains a hardness core, any sound checker for that core requires witness budget at least $2^{n-1}$, bounded action families admit polynomial-time exact checking, cutoff locality bounds docking structural rank by active-site and ligand coordinates, sampled docking admits theorem-backed exact/coarse winner preservation and inside-cutoff sufficiency under explicit gap and compatibility hypotheses, action-side symmetry breaking yields a canonical strict sampled winner with a uniform positive strict gap and no structural-rank increase, top-$k$ as well as near-tie regimes remain under certified control, and strict-dominance or multiplicative-separable scorer families collapse conformer dependence by making every coordinate set sufficient.

\item \textbf{Thermodynamic and Statistical Dimension:} the canonical exact-resolution problem assigns one binary acquisition channel to each degree of freedom, the resulting structural rank equals that channel count, the decision entropy is bounded by the same count, the Fisher-information dimension is the same structural rank, and under Landauer calibration exact-resolution cost is bounded below by both $\mathrm{DOF}(A)\,k_B T \ln 2$ and $k_B T H_{\mathrm{nats}}(D)$.

\item \textbf{Approximation, Geometry, and Concrete Scorer Families:} constrained molecular systems with $N$ atoms and $k$ independent holonomic constraints transport directly into the framework with effective dimension $3N-k$, binary constraint-status interface, and a Landauer-linear floor scaling with the remaining unconstrained coordinates. Continuous-to-grid Lipschitz control yields uniform exact/coarse approximation radii, large-cutoff lattice-tail bounds discharge the cutoff-locality hypothesis, finite sampled docking admits a canonical discrepancy radius and theorem-backed pruning certificates, finite sampled Lennard-Jones and Coulomb scorer families satisfy exact/coarse invariance criteria under explicit cutoff error and half-gap conditions, and the geometric execution layer proves exact phase-space volume preservation for Velocity-Verlet, a closed-form Lennard-Jones gradient, and exponential Ewald decay for long-range Coulomb splitting.

\item \textbf{Nonideal Exact Resolution:} theorem-level mismatch and residual witnesses force effective per-bit floors strictly above Landauer, explicit binary mismatch and two-state residual witnesses each yield an additive one-unit overhead above the Landauer floor, and finite-capacity substrates therefore have bounded lifetime and bounded entropy throughput.

\item \textbf{Convergence and Universal Consequences:} the rank-$1$ regime is simultaneously the one-coordinate regime, the tractable sufficiency regime, the coherent single-source regime, and the thermodynamic ground state; the same model yields the finite replication entropy gap and the finite-budget no-collapse theorem.

\item \textbf{Finite Physical Acquisition:} bounded systems admit finitely many acquisition events, bounded regions admit finite acquisition rates, acquisition is represented by discrete transition events, and exact resolution requires a sufficient coordinate set.

\item \textbf{Concrete Substrate Instantiation:} thresholded one-bit readouts and two-level atomic transition systems instantiate the canonical binary interface used by the theorem chain.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Physical significance.} Once finite acquisition is fixed as the physical interface, the degree-of-freedom count becomes simultaneously an interaction dimension, an entropy bound, and a Landauer-calibrated cost coordinate. The result concerns exact resolution structure in matter.

Informally: exact resolution must be paid for.
