\section{Introduction}\label{introduction}

This paper studies finite information-processing systems through a single structural parameter: degrees of freedom. The central result is the Five-Way Equivalence (Theorem~\ref{thm:five-way}): engineering leverage, epistemic coherence, structural rank, tractable sufficiency, and minimum thermodynamic cost all select the same regime, $\mathrm{DOF}=1$. The main contribution of this version is the mathematical-physics side of that equivalence: it turns a structural counting parameter into a calibrated entropy and energy law. All claims are verified in Lean~4 with zero \texttt{sorry} placeholders.

\subsection{Central Result}

\begin{theorem}[Five-Way Equivalence]\label{thm:five-way}
For any architecture $A$ with $\mathrm{Cap}(A) > 0$, the following are equivalent:
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\underbrace{\mathrm{DOF}(A) = 1}_{\text{single source}}
\;\iff\;
\underbrace{L(A) \text{ maximal}}_{\text{engineering}}
\;\iff\;
\underbrace{\mathrm{SSOT}(A)}_{\text{epistemics}} \\
&\iff\;
\underbrace{\mathrm{srank}(A) = 1}_{\text{information}}
\;\iff\;
\underbrace{\text{tractable sufficiency}}_{\text{complexity}}
\;\iff\;
\underbrace{\text{min thermo cost}}_{\text{physics}}
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{theorem}

Proved in Section~\ref{five-way-equivalence}. Machine-checked via \texttt{Leverage} $\to$ \texttt{DecisionQuotient} $\to$ \texttt{Ssot} $\to$ Mathlib.

\subsection{Contributions}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{DOF-Structural-Rank Identification (Theorem~5.3):} the canonical decision problem associated with an $n$-degree-of-freedom system has structural rank $n$.

\item \textbf{Tractability Boundary (Theorem~5.5):} rank-$1$ sufficiency checking is polynomial-time, while rank greater than one enters the coNP-hard regime in the companion structural-rank theory.

\item \textbf{Thermodynamic Selection Theorem (Theorem~5.7):} under Landauer calibration, degree of freedom above one forces strictly higher per-cycle decision cost, and the positive energy floor is unconditional.

\item \textbf{England Replication Inequality (Theorem~\ref{thm:england}):} $\Delta S_{\min}(k) - \Delta S_{\min}(1) \geq k_B \ln k$. The proof reduces the entropy gap to counting via $k \leq 2^{k-1}$. \leanmeta{\LH{L45}}

\item \textbf{Five-Way Convergence (Theorem~\ref{thm:five-way}):} five independently motivated frameworks select the same structural regime, making the single-source point simultaneously optimal for leverage, coherence, rank, tractability, and thermodynamic cost.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{What is new.} \texttt{AbstractClassSystem} and \texttt{Ssot} establish two of the five equivalences. This paper closes the physics-facing half of the chain: it identifies structural rank as the information coordinate of DOF, proves thermodynamic selection under Landauer calibration, and derives the England-style replication inequality from finite counting.

\subsection{Scope}

The focus of this manuscript is not software methodology per se, but the mathematical structure linking leverage, structural rank, complexity, and thermodynamic cost. Reliability-style and edit-cost consequences are kept because they expose the same parameter in a more familiar discrete language. A separate software-engineering split develops the architectural decision framework and case-study material in its own venue-facing form.
